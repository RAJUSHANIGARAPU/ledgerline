1. Who is responsible

The controller for personal data processed in this installation of Ledgerline is [CONTROLLER NAME], [ADDRESS], KvK [KVK NUMBER]. Contact for privacy questions: [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL].

2. What data is processed and why

  • Account data of users: email address, password hash (argon2), role, sign-in and change events. Purpose: access control and an audit trail.
  • Contact data of customers and suppliers: name, email, address, VAT number, KvK number, IBAN. Purpose: issuing and booking invoices and matching bank payments.
  • Bookkeeping records: invoices, journal entries, bank transactions, attachments. Purpose: keeping the administration as the law requires.
  • Pilot feedback: the text you type into the Feedback form, with your user account and the administration it was sent from, plus a log of when you sent it. Purpose: improving the service during the pilot. Do not put invoices, bank details or other people's personal data in it.

3. Legal basis

[TO BE CONFIRMED BY THE CONTROLLER] Typically: performance of a contract (invoicing customers), a legal obligation (the fiscal duty to keep records) and legitimate interest (security logging).

4. Retention

Bookkeeping records are kept for 7 years (10 years for records about real estate), counted from when the data stops being current. Personal data that is not part of a bookkeeping record is removed or anonymised on request. When an administration is offboarded, its data is deleted from the live system; copies remain in backups until the backups expire (see section 5).

4a. Pilot feedback

[NEEDS REVIEW BY A LAWYER] While this installation runs as a pilot, the operator uses the feedback you send through the Feedback link to improve the service. The text is stored exactly as you wrote it, together with your account, the administration and the time.

Legal basis: [NEEDS REVIEW] legitimate interest of the operator in improving the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you can object. Retention: feedback is deleted with the administration when the pilot ends and the administration is offboarded. Copies in backups disappear when the backup expires (see section 5). Who can see it: the operator, and the owner of the administration, who finds the feedback of all users of that administration in the administration export. It is not shared with anyone else and not used for advertising. Access, export and deletion: ask the operator at [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]; this is handled by hand.

5. Recipients and hosting

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database on infrastructure chosen by the operator: [HOSTING PROVIDER]. A data processing agreement with the hosting provider is needed where the provider can access the data. Encrypted backups are kept on the operator's server and at [BACKUP STORAGE PROVIDER]: local copies for [30] days, off-site copies as daily, weekly, monthly and yearly snapshots, the oldest of which can be about [8] years old. A single administration cannot be removed from a backup; after a restore the operator deletes offboarded administrations again. The application loads no external scripts, fonts or trackers. It makes no calls to third-party services, except the optional document extraction described in section 8 when the operator has switched it on.

6. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, export, restriction and erasure, and you can object to processing. The operator can export all data held on a contact and can anonymise a contact. Erasure is limited where a legal retention duty applies: the bookkeeping records stay until the retention period ends. Complaints can be sent to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed with argon2, sessions use signed cookies, forms are protected against cross-site request forgery, and the application sets restrictive security headers. See SECURITY.md in the project.

8. Document extraction (optional)

[KEEP THIS SECTION ONLY IF THE OPERATOR ENABLED EXTRACTION; OTHERWISE DELETE IT] When document extraction is enabled, a received invoice or receipt that could not be read directly is sent to Anthropic (provider of the Claude models) so that its fields can be read. Anthropic acts as processor on behalf of the controller. The document is sent as is and can contain personal data of suppliers, such as names, addresses and bank details. Nothing is sent when the feature is off, and the result is only a proposal that a person checks before anything is booked.
