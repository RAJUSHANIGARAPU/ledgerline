Ledgerline sets no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies and loads nothing from third parties.

Why there is no consent banner

Our understanding is that the Dutch cookie rule (Telecommunicatiewet, article 11.7a) exempts cookies that are strictly necessary to provide a service the user asked for, such as keeping a session and protecting a form. Because only such cookies are used, no consent banner is shown. If you add analytics or other cookies to your installation, this changes and you need to revisit it.
